Privacy Notice

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Made for More respects your privacy. This notice explains how we use the information you provide when registering for a live workshop.

Information we collect

We may collect your name, email address, WhatsApp number, profession, and workshop selection. Paddle processes payment information when you pay. Made for More does not receive or store your full card details.

Website journey data

We use a first-party, random device ID to understand the pages and sections visitors view, time spent, scroll behaviour, and workshop-registration steps. We do not use fingerprinting or store your full browser user agent. We save form details before submission only when you agree on the registration form that we may securely save them to help you finish your registration and send workshop information.

How we use it

We use your information to process your registration, send workshop access and important updates, provide support, process eligible refunds, understand where people leave the registration journey, and improve workshop operations.

Who receives it

We use Paddle to process payments and Cloudflare to securely store our website journey and registration data. We also use the Meta Pixel to measure website visits and advertising actions such as registrations and purchases. Paddle acts as the payment provider and handles payment information under its own privacy terms. We do not sell your personal information.

How long we keep it

We keep registration information only for as long as reasonably necessary for workshop delivery, support, refunds, record keeping, and legal obligations.

Your choices

You may ask us to correct or delete your registration information where applicable by emailing shanjairajdev@gmail.com.
